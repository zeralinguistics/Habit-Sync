HABIT SYNC: PRIVACY

Habit Sync is a personal app. Everything you log stays on this phone.

What is stored

Your weight, food, workouts, rehab check-ins, pain scores, steps and sleep are saved in the app's own storage on this device. There is no account and no server.

Health Connect (Samsung Health)

If you tap Connect, the app asks Health Connect for read-only access to steps, active calories, sleep, weight and workouts. It reads them to fill in your day. It never writes to Health Connect and never sends the data anywhere. You can remove access at any time in Health Connect settings.

Reminders

Reminders are ordinary notifications scheduled on the phone. They need no internet and send nothing anywhere.

Updates

The Android app asks GitHub once a day whether a newer version was published. It reads only the public release name. Nothing about you is sent.

What leaves the phone

Nothing, unless you choose to export a backup file or copy your physio sheet and send it yourself.

Deleting your data

Use Forge, then Data vault, then Reset everything. A reset keeps one undo copy on the phone so a slip can be reversed; uninstalling the app removes everything, undo copy included.
